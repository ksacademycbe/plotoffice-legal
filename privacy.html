Effective date: [DD Month YYYY] · Version 1.0 · Applies to the PLOT OFFICE app for iPhone and iPad and to any PLOT OFFICE apps for other platforms that reference this policy.

The short version

  • PLOT OFFICE is an offline-first tool. Your projects, layout geometry, plot inventory, customer records, schedules and settings are stored on your device, in an encrypted app folder.
  • We operate no servers that receive your data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking technology, and it does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because it never tracks.
  • Cloud backup, when you turn it on, goes to a cloud account that you own and sign in to yourself (iCloud on Apple devices; Google Drive or a comparable service where offered on other platforms). We cannot read those backups.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • DXF drawings you import are read entirely on your device. The original file is not kept; only the geometry needed for your layout is stored.
  • PLOT OFFICE is distributed worldwide through App Store storefronts outside Europe. European regional frameworks such as the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR and the Swiss FADP do not apply to this app because it is not offered in those territories. See section 12.

2. Information the app stores on your device

3. DXF drawings

When you import a DXF file, the app parses it on your device to find closed plot boundaries, labels and reference lines. Only the resulting geometry, labels and a per-drawing handle used to match later revisions are saved. The original drawing file is not retained by the app. Exported DXF files are generated on your device and handed to the destination you choose through the system share sheet.

4. Cloud backup you control

Backup is optional and off until you leave it on. It uses a cloud account that belongs to you:

  • iCloud (iPhone and iPad). The app writes a single backup record to the private CloudKit database of the Apple Account signed in on your device, in the container iCloud.com.ksacademy.PlotOffice. Private CloudKit data is encrypted by Apple and is not accessible to us. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy govern that storage. Turning backup off stops new uploads; it does not delete the cloud copy, which you can remove from iCloud storage settings.
  • Google Drive or other personal cloud services (where offered on other platforms). If a PLOT OFFICE app on another platform offers backup to a service such as Google Drive, it writes to a folder in your own account after you sign in with that provider. The provider's terms and privacy policy apply. We receive no credentials and no copy of the data.
  • Optional synchronisation endpoint. The app can be configured to push a copy of your workspace to a synchronisation service only if you or your organisation explicitly connect one and sign in. The current App Store release offers no such service operated by us and makes no such connection by default. If you connect a third-party or self-hosted endpoint, that operator's policies govern the data it receives.

Before the app replaces local records with a cloud copy, it keeps a local recovery copy of the replaced version, which you can export from Settings. Recovery copies live on your device only.

5. Purchases

PLOT OFFICE Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system as a lifetime purchase or a yearly subscription. Apple processes payment and holds your payment details; we never receive card or billing information. To unlock Pro, the app checks the signed purchase record that Apple stores on your device using StoreKit. We do not run an account system and do not link purchases to any identity we hold. Refunds and subscription management are handled through Apple.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics, usage statistics, or telemetry of any kind, first- or third-party.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, or ad networks.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no use of the App Tracking Transparency framework because there is nothing to request.
  • No background location, background network monitoring, or push notifications.
  • No remote servers operated by us that receive your records. The only network connections the app makes are to Apple services you use (iCloud, App Store) and to a synchronisation endpoint you explicitly connect.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. There is nothing to sell or share.

8. Sharing and export

When you export a CSV, DXF or JSON file, print a brochure, send a WhatsApp or phone link, or share a project, data leaves the app only to the destination you pick in that moment, through standard system features. We are not party to those transfers.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Device data remains until you delete a record in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's Documents folder, Keychain items the app created, and its preferences.
  • iCloud backups remain until you delete them from iCloud (Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › PLOT OFFICE) or replace them from another device.
  • Pro entitlement records are regenerated from Apple's purchase history and can be cleared by uninstalling.

10. Security

Records are stored with iOS Data Protection and written atomically so an interrupted write cannot corrupt an earlier copy. Any sign-in tokens for an optional synchronisation endpoint are stored in the iOS Keychain, restricted to the device they were created on. No method of storage is perfectly secure; keep your device passcode and iCloud account protected, and keep your own exported backups of important data.

11. Children

PLOT OFFICE is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app collects no information from anyone.
